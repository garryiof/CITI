# Pass W — Workflow grouping

Group the extracted actions into complete, topic-coherent end-to-end workflows a reader would follow across the policy lifecycle (for example: disclose reportable accounts and maintain approved brokers, obtain trade pre-clearance and execute within window, observe prohibited transactions and blackout rules, request private investment approval, complete annual certification and training, escalate policy violations or exceptions).
Rules:
- Use only the action labels provided, copied verbatim.
- Cover EVERY extracted action in at least one workflow; do not drop detailed steps, deadlines, prohibitions, or recordkeeping tasks.
- Keep related sequential steps together in rich multi-step workflows (typically 4 to 12 steps when supported by the actions) rather than splitting closely related tasks into 1- or 2-step fragments.
- Order each workflow's action_labels chronologically from trigger/initiation -> review/approval/check -> execution/prohibition -> post-activity reporting/recordkeeping.
- An action may appear in more than one workflow when relevant.
- Give every workflow a clear title, a one-sentence summary, and the trigger that starts it, all grounded in the action descriptions.
- Do not invent actions, workflows, or steps that are not supported by the provided actions.
